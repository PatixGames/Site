Privacy Policy - Mosaicrisp

Effective date: 26.10.2026

Ilya Sakhno, trading as Patix Games ("we", "our", or "us"), operates the mobile game Mosaicrisp (the "Game") and is the controller of the personal data described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Game, how it is used, how it may be shared, and what choices you have. The Game does not require or offer user accounts, and we do not run our own servers for the Game: your progress is stored on your device.

The Short Version (for Younger Players)

  • We never ask for your name, e-mail, photos or where you live. You do not need an account.
  • The Game asks your age once, so it can protect younger players. It keeps only an age group, on your phone.
  • If you are under 18, the Game does not track how you play, and its ads are not chosen from what you do in other apps.
  • If you are under 18, ads are limited to ones made for everyone (rated G). Watching an ad for a reward is always your choice.
  • You never have to buy anything. Purchases go through Google Play, which can ask a parent first.
  • Questions, or want something deleted? Ask a parent to write to us at patix.games.sup@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Depending on your choices, age, region and device settings, we and our service providers may collect or receive the following types of information:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country or region, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers and other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob only, and not for players treated as children), the Firebase app instance ID, and identifiers generated by the advertising SDK.
  • Gameplay and usage data, such as level starts and results, level duration, boosters used, album completions, shop product views, ad requests, ad views and rewarded ad completions.
  • Purchase information, such as purchased item identifiers, purchase status and transaction identifiers provided by Google Play Billing.
  • Approximate location, such as country or region, derived from your IP address by our service providers.
  • Consent and privacy choices, such as your advertising and analytics consent status.
  • Your age band. On first launch the Game asks for your age in years. Only the resulting age band (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over) is stored, on your device only; the age itself is not kept or sent anywhere.

Through the Game we do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health data, government identification numbers, or payment card details. The Game does not include a crash reporting SDK; if you choose to share diagnostics with Google in your Android settings, Google may provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send, as described under Contacting Us by Email.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the Game.
  • Deliver and restore purchased content.
  • Show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch and ads between levels, when advertising is permitted.
  • Measure ad performance, limit how often ads are shown, prevent fraud and comply with advertising platform requirements.
  • Analyze how adult players use the Game in order to improve level difficulty, balance and the Game, if analytics is permitted.
  • Apply age-appropriate settings to ads, analytics and offers.
  • Comply with legal obligations, platform rules and security requirements.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are: your consent (personalized advertising and, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics); performance of our contract with you (providing the Game and purchased content); and our legitimate interests (non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and keeping the Game working), where consent is not required.

Advertising and Consent

The Game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch and, after some levels, full-screen ads between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We do not use other advertising networks or ad mediation. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G) for players who declared they are under 18, and to content suitable for most audiences with parental guidance (PG) for adults. Ads for gambling, betting and alcohol are blocked for everyone. Purchasing "No Ads" removes ads between levels; rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your choices through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. In the United States, a privacy message lets you opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising and of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal data; if you opt out, ads are requested with Google's Restricted Data Processing.

You can change your choices at any time from the Game's Settings screen (Privacy options), where your region requires it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location and ad interaction data to provide and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the Game. Analytics is used only for players who declared that they are 18 or over. It is off by default and does not start before the age and consent steps are complete. It does not collect the Android Advertising ID, is not linked to advertising, and the events contain only game values (such as a level identifier or a product identifier), never your name or anything you type.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics stays off until you give consent, and nothing is collected or sent if you do not; you can withdraw consent through Privacy options. Elsewhere, analytics is used for adult players to improve the Game. If the Game cannot tell your region (for example, on a first launch without an internet connection), it applies the rules of the European Economic Area. Events that happen while analytics is off are discarded, not stored for later.

Age and Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. On first launch the Game asks for your age without suggesting any answer, and applies the following settings:

  • Under 13: ads are requested with child-directed treatment (no Advertising ID, no personalized or remarketing ads) and Restricted Data Processing; Firebase Analytics is not used; no personalized offers are shown.
  • 13–15: in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, players are treated as under the age of digital consent: no consent form is shown and ads are requested with child-directed treatment. Elsewhere, ads are requested with teen treatment. In every region ads are requested with Restricted Data Processing. No personalized advertising, no sale or sharing of personal data, no Firebase Analytics and no personalized offers.
  • 16–17: ads are requested with teen treatment and Restricted Data Processing. No personalized advertising, no sale or sharing of personal data, no Firebase Analytics and no personalized offers.
  • 18 and over: standard settings, subject to your consent choices.

The age band is used only for these protections, never for targeting. Purchases through Google Play are subject to Google Play's own age checks and parental controls.

Notice for parents (COPPA). For players who declare they are under 13, the Game collects no personal information other than persistent identifiers generated by the advertising SDK, device information and approximate location (from the IP address) used by Google AdMob only to support the internal operations of the Game: to serve contextual (non-personalized) ads, to limit how often ads are shown, to measure ad delivery and to prevent fraud. These identifiers are not used to contact any individual, to build a profile of a child, or for interest-based or remarketing advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play provides us with limited purchase information, such as the item identifier, purchase status and transaction identifier, so that we can deliver the item and restore permanent purchases.

Booster packs are consumable items. They are stored in your game save on your device, are protected by Android backup if you have it enabled, and cannot be restored after reinstalling without a backup. Permanent items (such as No Ads, speed upgrades, the extra slot, skins, material packs and the Supporter Pack) are restored from Google Play through Restore purchases in Settings.

Refunds. You can request a refund through Google Play. You can also write to us at patix.games.sup@gmail.com: we grant refund requests made within 14 days of the purchase and consider later ones case by case. A refunded permanent item is removed from the Game the next time the Game checks your purchases with Google Play; booster packs already added are not taken back.

Data Stored on Your Device and Backup

Your game progress, settings, age band, consent status and purchase records are stored locally on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this data may be backed up to your Google account by Google and restored when you reinstall the Game. We do not have access to these backups.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing — platform services and in-app purchases (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — privacy and consent messages.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — analytics for adult players (Firebase privacy information).
  • Unity In-App Purchasing — connection to Google Play Billing. It does not collect personal data in the version used by the Game.

Data Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, and with authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users or the security of the Game. We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out as described under Advertising and Consent. We never sell or share personal data of players who declared they are under 18, and never use it for targeted advertising.

Contacting Us by Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to include. We use this only to answer you and to fix problems in the Game, on the basis of your request and our legitimate interest in providing support. We delete the correspondence within 2 years after the conversation ends, unless we need to keep it longer to handle a legal claim.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device stays there until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Firebase Analytics data is retained for the period set in our Google Analytics settings and then deleted automatically. Google retains advertising and purchase data under its own policies. Emails are kept as described under Contacting Us by Email.

Because the Game has no accounts and we hold no data of our own on servers, deleting the Game's data on your device removes your game data and resets the Firebase app instance ID. Analytics data is not linked to your name or email address, so we usually cannot find it from your message alone; Firebase deletes it automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
