Privacy Policy

Effective date: 01.06.2026

Patix Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Car Sort Jam (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Game.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account to play the Game.

Depending on the features and third-party services used in the Game, we and our service providers may collect the following types of information:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country, and app version.
  • Identifiers, such as advertising ID, app instance ID, or other device identifiers.
  • Usage data, such as gameplay events, level progress, session duration, interactions with ads, and general app activity.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs, performance data, and technical error reports.
  • Purchase information, if you make in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • Approximate location, such as country or region, usually derived from your IP address.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health data, or financial information directly from users.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Game.
  • Save or restore game progress, where applicable.
  • Improve gameplay, balance levels, fix bugs, and optimize performance.
  • Show ads, if advertising is enabled in the Game.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud.
  • Analyze how users interact with the Game.
  • Process in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • Comply with legal obligations and platform requirements.

The Game may use third-party services that collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. These services may include:

Advertising

The Game may display ads provided by third-party advertising networks. Advertising partners may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve ads.

Depending on your device settings and region, you may be able to limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID.

If the Game offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your full payment card details. Google may provide us with limited purchase-related information, such as transaction status and purchased item identifiers, to unlock content and manage purchases.

We may share information with:

We do not sell personal information directly to third parties.

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
