Privacy Policy - Clouds on Payroll

Effective date: 13.10.2026

Ilya Sakhno, trading as Patix Games ("we", "our", or "us"), operates the mobile game Clouds on Payroll (the "Game") and is the controller of the personal data described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Game, how it is used, how it may be shared, and what choices you have. The Game does not require or offer user accounts, and we do not run our own servers for the Game: your progress is stored on your device.

Information We Collect

Depending on your choices, age, region and device settings, we and our service providers may collect or receive the following types of information:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country or region, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers and other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, the Firebase app instance ID, and identifiers generated by the advertising SDK.
  • Gameplay and usage data, such as progress, session activity, gameplay events, shop and offer views, ad requests, ad views and rewarded ad completions.
  • Purchase information, such as purchased item identifiers, purchase status and transaction identifiers provided by Google Play Billing.
  • Approximate location, such as country or region, derived from your IP address by our service providers.
  • Consent and privacy choices, such as your advertising and analytics consent status.
  • Your age band. On first launch the Game asks for your year of birth. Only the resulting age band (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over) is stored, on your device only; the year itself is not kept or sent anywhere.

Through the Game we do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health data, government identification numbers, or payment card details. The Game does not include a crash reporting SDK; if you choose to share diagnostics with Google in your Android settings, Google may provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics through Google Play Console. If you write to us, we receive what you send, as described under Support Emails.

Support Emails

The Support button in the Game's Settings opens your email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft contains the app version, device model, operating system version and language, so that we can understand your problem; you see the whole message and can edit or remove anything before sending it. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you write to us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to include. We use this only to answer you and to fix problems in the Game, on the basis of your request and our legitimate interest in providing support. We delete the correspondence within 2 years after the conversation ends, unless we need to keep it longer to handle a legal claim.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the Game.
  • Deliver and restore purchased content.
  • Show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, when advertising is permitted.
  • Measure ad performance, prevent fraud and comply with advertising platform requirements.
  • Analyze how players use the Game in order to improve balance, fix issues and improve the Game, if analytics is permitted.
  • Apply age-appropriate settings to ads and analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations, platform rules and security requirements.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are: your consent (personalized advertising and, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics); performance of our contract with you (providing the Game and purchased content); and our legitimate interests (non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and keeping the Game working), where consent is not required.

Advertising and Consent

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. We do not use other advertising networks or ad mediation.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your choices through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ad is requested. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. In the United States, a privacy message lets you opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising and of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal data; if you opt out, ads are requested with Google's Restricted Data Processing.

You can change your choices at any time from the Game's Settings screen (Privacy options). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location and ad interaction data to provide and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the Game. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics collection stays off until you give consent, and nothing is collected or sent if you do not. Elsewhere, analytics is used to improve the Game, and you can withdraw through Privacy options where available.

Age and Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. On first launch the Game asks for the year of birth without suggesting any answer, and applies the following settings:

  • Under 13: ads are requested as child-directed (no personalized ads), Firebase Analytics is not used and no personalized offers are shown.
  • 13–17: no personalized advertising and no sale or sharing of personal data; in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, players under the age of digital consent are treated as such for advertising consent.
  • 18 and over: standard settings, subject to your consent choices.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play provides us with limited purchase information, such as the item identifier, purchase status and transaction identifier, so that we can deliver the item and restore permanent purchases.

Consumable items (such as tickets and folders) are stored in your game save on your device. They are protected by Android backup if you have it enabled, but cannot be restored after reinstalling without a backup. Permanent items are restored from Google Play.

Data Stored on Your Device and Backup

Your game progress, settings, age band and purchase records are stored locally on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this data may be backed up to your Google account by Google and restored when you reinstall the Game. We do not have access to these backups.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing — platform services and in-app purchases (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — privacy and consent messages.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — analytics (Firebase privacy information).
  • Unity In-App Purchasing — connection to Google Play Billing. It does not collect personal data in the version used by the Game.

Data Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, and with authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users or the security of the Game. We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out as described under Advertising and Consent.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device stays there until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Firebase Analytics data is retained for the period set in our Google Analytics settings and then deleted automatically. Google retains advertising and purchase data under its own policies. Support emails are kept as described under Support Emails.

Because the Game has no accounts and we hold no data of our own on servers, deleting the Game's data on your device removes your game data. Analytics data is not linked to your name or email address, so we usually cannot find it from your message alone. You can stop analytics collection at any time through Privacy options in the Game's Settings where available, and Firebase deletes analytics data automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or others), you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal data, to opt out of targeted advertising, "sale" or "sharing", and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our answer with the subject "Appeal"; we will respond within the time required by law.
